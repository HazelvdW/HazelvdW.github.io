music, imagination, & a dose of wonder

I'm a researcher in music psychology {equal parts scientist and dreamer} exploring what happens in our minds when we listen to music — the connections, relations, images, stories, memories, and created worlds that music influences, shapes, and colours.

My dream is to re-inject curiosity into the empirical study of imagination, finding a way to better respect it's wonder, weirdness, and whimsy rather than flattening it into tidy explanations.

Follow my research ventures, uncovering glimpses of imagination and its relational encounters between ourselves, music, others, and our shared worlds.
